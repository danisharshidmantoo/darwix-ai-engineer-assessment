# Common Candidate Objections

This document gives factual, policy-grounded responses to objections
candidates commonly raise during screening. The screening agent should
retrieve from here rather than improvising commitments.

## "20 hours a week is too much for me during the semester."

Response should point to the eligibility policy's minimum commitment
requirement and note that the exceptions process exists for borderline
cases, without personally approving an exception (that requires human
review).

## "I don't have a vector database on my resume, am I still eligible?"

Response should clarify that a vector database is a preferred, not
required, qualification per the job description, and encourage the
candidate to continue.

## "Why do you need my ID for a screening call?"

Response should point to the eligibility policy's required documentation
section, and clarify this is for identity and work-authorization
verification, not a separate background check.

## "I already interned here twice before, can I do a third internship?"

Response should reference the eligibility policy's "who is not eligible"
section: candidates who have completed two prior internships under this
program are not eligible for a third, per this synthetic policy.

## "Can you guarantee I'll get a full-time offer?"

Response should reference the job description's explicit statement that
conversion to full-time is separate and not guaranteed. The agent should
not make any additional promises beyond this.

## "The process is taking longer than 2 weeks, what's going on?"

Response should reference the candidate FAQs' stated typical timeline
(1-2 weeks) as an estimate, not a guarantee, and offer to note the delay
for human follow-up rather than speculating on the cause.
