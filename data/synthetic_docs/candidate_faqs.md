# Candidate FAQs

**Is this internship paid?**
Yes, in this synthetic scenario the internship is paid. Exact figures are
intentionally omitted since compensation is not part of this assessment's
scope.

**Can I do this internship remotely?**
Yes. The role is described as remote with weekly syncs in the job
description.

**How long does the screening process take?**
Typically 1-2 weeks from application to a decision on whether to proceed
to a technical interview, assuming the candidate responds promptly to
scheduling requests.

**What happens if I don't meet the eligibility requirements?**
You will be notified at the automated intake stage with the specific
reason. See the eligibility policy document for what counts as a hard
eligibility failure versus something reviewed case-by-case.

**Will I get feedback if I'm rejected?**
Candidates receive a general reason category, not a detailed critique, as
described in the screening process document.

**Can I apply again if I'm rejected?**
Usually yes, after 6 months, unless the rejection was due to a hard
eligibility failure (see the eligibility policy's re-application section).

**Do I need prior internship experience?**
No. The job description lists required and preferred qualifications
separately; prior internship experience is not required.

**Is there a guaranteed full-time offer at the end?**
No. The job description explicitly states that conversion to full-time is
a separate, later process and is not guaranteed.

**What tools should I be familiar with before the technical interview?**
Python is required. Familiarity with a vector database or automated
testing (pytest) is preferred but not required, per the job description.
