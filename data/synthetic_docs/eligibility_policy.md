# Internship Eligibility Policy

## Who can apply

- Applicants must be enrolled in, or have graduated within the last 12
  months from, an undergraduate or graduate program.
- Applicants must be legally authorized to work in the country the
  internship is based in, or eligible under a recognized student work
  authorization scheme.
- Applicants must be able to commit at least 20 hours per week for the
  full duration of the internship.

## Who is not eligible

- Applicants who have already completed two prior internships at the
  company under this program (synthetic rule, for demonstration only).
- Applicants who cannot provide proof of enrollment or graduation when
  requested.
- Applicants applying from a location where the company has no legal
  entity to employ interns (synthetic constraint).

## Required documentation

- Proof of current enrollment or a graduation certificate/transcript.
- A government-issued ID for identity verification.
- Work authorization documentation, if applicable to the applicant's
  location.

## Exceptions process

Eligibility exceptions (for example, a borderline enrollment date) are
reviewed by the hiring team on a case-by-case basis. Candidates should
raise exception requests during the initial screening call rather than
after an offer has been made.

## Re-application policy

Candidates who are not selected may re-apply after 6 months, unless the
rejection reason was a hard eligibility failure (e.g. lack of work
authorization), in which case re-application is only appropriate once
that condition changes.
