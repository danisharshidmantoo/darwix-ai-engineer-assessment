# End-to-End Hiring Process

This document describes the full pipeline a candidate moves through,
from application to offer. It is broader than the screening process
document, which only covers the automated/early stages in detail.

## Pipeline stages

1. **Application** — candidate submits resume and short answers.
2. **Automated screening** — eligibility checks and structured
   conversation (see screening_process.md).
3. **Recruiter review** — human review of the screening transcript.
4. **Technical interview** — a 45-60 minute session with an engineer,
   covering a practical coding exercise relevant to the role.
5. **Team fit conversation** — a shorter, informal conversation with the
   team the intern would join.
6. **Decision** — hiring team decides to extend an offer, waitlist, or
   reject.
7. **Offer and onboarding** — offer details are sent, and if accepted,
   onboarding paperwork begins.

## Timelines (synthetic, for demonstration only)

- Application to automated screening result: within 3 business days.
- Screening to technical interview scheduling: within 5 business days.
- Technical interview to decision: within 7 business days.

## Roles involved

- **Screening agent (automated):** handles Stage 2 only. Does not make
  hiring decisions.
- **Recruiter:** owns Stage 3 and communicates outcomes to candidates.
- **Interviewing engineer:** owns Stage 4, provides a structured
  evaluation.
- **Hiring manager:** makes the final decision in Stage 6.

## What candidates should not expect from the automated stages

- The automated screening agent does not make or imply a final decision.
- The automated screening agent does not skip stages or fast-track
  candidates, regardless of how a conversation goes.
