# Darwix ProtectPlus Bancassurance Plan

## Product Overview

Darwix ProtectPlus is a synthetic bancassurance life insurance package offered
exclusively through partner bank referrals to account holders. It combines basic
life protection with optional health and accident riders to ensure comprehensive
family financial security.

## Core Coverage and Benefits

1. **Basic Life Insurance Benefit (Sum Assured)**
   - Provides a guaranteed lump-sum cash benefit to designated beneficiaries
     upon the untimely passing of the insured.
   - Available Sum Assured tiers:
     - Tier A: ₱500,000 (Entry level)
     - Tier B: ₱1,000,000 (Standard family protection)
     - Tier C: ₱2,000,000 (Comprehensive protection)

2. **Accidental Death and Disablement Benefit (ADB)**
   - Doubles the basic sum assured (200% payout) if demise is due to a covered
     accident.
   - Includes partial dismemberment cash benefits based on the standard
     indemnity schedule.

3. **Optional Add-on Riders**
   - **Critical Illness Rider (CI Rider):** Lump-sum cash advance equal to 50%
     or 100% of sum assured upon diagnosis of any of 36 covered major critical
     illnesses (e.g., cancer, stroke, heart attack). Subject to a 90-day waiting
     period from policy issuance.
   - **Daily Hospital Income Benefit (HIB Rider):** Daily cash assistance of
     ₱1,500 to ₱3,000 per day of hospital confinement (minimum 24 hours
     confinement, up to 100 days per year).

## Eligibility and Issue Ages

- **Insured Issue Age:** 18 to 60 years old (renewable up to age 70).
- **Policy Owner:** Must be at least 18 years old.
- **Residency:** Filipino citizens or foreign nationals with permanent residency /
  valid Alien Certificate of Registration (ACR) residing in the Philippines.

## What is NOT Covered (General Exclusions)

- Pre-existing medical conditions not disclosed during the application.
- Self-inflicted injuries or suicide within the first two (2) years of policy
  inception or reinstatement.
- Hazardous extreme sports (e.g., skydiving, scuba diving below 40 meters)
  unless officially declared and rated.
