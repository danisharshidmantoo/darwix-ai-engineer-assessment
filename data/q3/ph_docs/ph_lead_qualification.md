# Bancassurance Lead Qualification & Referral Guidelines

## Lead Intake and Source

Bancassurance leads originate primarily from partner bank referrals when account
holders visit a branch, transact online, or request an initial insurance
screening call. Automated voice screening qualifies leads before transferring
them to a licensed Bancassurance Financial Advisor.

## Mandatory Qualification Checklist

To qualify for Darwix ProtectPlus, the candidate/client must satisfy all four (4)
baseline requirements:

1. **Age Requirement:**
   - Must be between 18 and 60 years old at the time of application.
   - Applicants age 61 and above are not eligible for new ProtectPlus policies
     (must be referred to specialized senior plans).

2. **Residency and ID Verification:**
   - Must reside in the Philippines.
   - Must be able to present at least one (1) valid Philippine government-issued ID
     (e.g., Philippine Passport, UMID, Driver's License, SSS ID, or National ID / PhilID).

3. **Bank Relationship / Referral Status:**
   - Must have an active deposit account or credit card with the partner bank to
     enable Auto-Debit Arrangement (ADA) premium deductions.

4. **Simplified Medical Declaration:**
   - Must confirm they are in general good health.
   - Must not have been hospitalized for more than 14 consecutive days or advised
     to undergo major surgery within the last 12 months.

## Disqualification Criteria (Hard Exclusions)

- Applicants younger than 18 or older than 60 years old.
- Non-residents or individuals living overseas permanently (OFWs must apply through
  designated overseas portal with consular notarization).
- Individuals diagnosed with terminal illness or active dialysis treatment.

## Lead Priority Tiers

- **Tier 1 (High Priority):** Existing depositors with active savings accounts
  requesting Critical Illness rider protection or family coverage.
- **Tier 2 (Standard):** Inquiries regarding premium quotations, payment options,
  or general policy information.
- **Tier 3 (Service / Escalation):** Existing policyholders inquiring about claims,
  reinstatement of lapsed policies, or complex endorsements.
