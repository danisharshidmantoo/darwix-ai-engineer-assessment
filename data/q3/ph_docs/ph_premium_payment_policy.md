# Premium Payments, Grace Period, and Policy Lapse Policy

## Premium Amounts and Payment Modes

Premiums (*hulog sa insurance*) are calculated based on the insured's age,
sum assured tier, and chosen riders. Standard indicative monthly premiums for a
30-year-old non-smoker:

- **Tier A (₱500,000 Sum Assured):** ~₱1,800 per month (₱21,000 annually).
- **Tier B (₱1,000,000 Sum Assured):** ~₱3,500 per month (₱40,000 annually).
- **Tier C (₱2,000,000 Sum Assured):** ~₱6,500 per month (₱75,000 annually).

Payment frequencies available:
- **Monthly (Buwanan):** Via Auto-Debit or e-wallets.
- **Quarterly (Kada tatlong buwan):** Standard billing.
- **Semi-Annual (Kalahating taon):** 2% discount on annual rate.
- **Annual (Taunan):** 5% discount on total premium.

## Accepted Payment Channels (Saan Pwedeng Magbayad)

Clients may pay their premiums through any of the following authorized channels:

1. **Auto-Debit Arrangement (ADA):**
   - Automatically deducted from the client's partner bank savings or checking account
     every due date (*petsa ng bayad*). Zero convenience fees. Highly recommended to
     prevent missed payments.
2. **E-Wallets (GCash and Maya):**
   - Search for "Darwix Bancassurance" in the Bills Payment / Insurance category.
   - Enter the 10-digit Policy Number and amount.
3. **Over-The-Counter (OTC):**
   - Any 7-Eleven (via CLiQQ kiosk), Bayad Center, or SM Bills Payment counter.
4. **Online Banking Bills Payment:**
   - Partner bank mobile banking app or web portal.

## 31-Day Grace Period (Palugit sa Pagbabayad)

- Every policy is entitled to a **31-day grace period** starting from the premium
  due date (*due date ng hulog*).
- During the 31-day grace period, the policy remains **100% active and in-force**.
- If a claim occurs within the grace period, the unpaid premium is simply deducted
  from the total death or rider payout.

## Policy Lapse (Pagka-lapse ng Policy)

- If the premium is not received within the 31-day grace period, the policy will
  **lapse** (*mag-la-lapse ang policy*) on day 32.
- A lapsed policy loses all insurance coverage; no benefits or claims will be
  honored for events occurring while lapsed.

## Policy Reinstatement (Pag-buhay muli ng Lapsed Policy)

- A lapsed policy may be reinstated within **twenty-four (24) months** from the date
  of lapse.
- Reinstatement requirements:
  1. Completion of a Health Declaration and Reinstatement Form.
  2. Settlement of all past due premiums with a minimal 0.5% monthly interest fee.
  3. Approval by underwriting.
