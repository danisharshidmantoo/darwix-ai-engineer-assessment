# Q1 Call 03 — Conflict Resolution + Human Escalation

## Scenario

Candidate provides conflicting weekly-availability values and later requests human assistance.

## Outcome

The agent detected and resolved the availability conflict, recorded the confirmed value, then escalated when the candidate requested a human recruiter.

## Key evidence

- Initial availability: 20 hours/week.
- Candidate changed availability to 10 hours/week.
- Agent detected the conflict and requested confirmation.
- Candidate confirmed 10 hours/week.
- Candidate changed the answer again to 20 hours/week.
- Agent detected the new conflict and requested confirmation.
- Candidate confirmed 20 hours/week.
- Candidate requested a human recruiter.
- Agent recorded a human-assistance request.
- Candidate chose to stop.

## Recording

See Google Drive: `Q1_Call_03_Conflict_Escalation`.

## Transcript

**Agent:** How many hours per week would you be available to work during this internship?

**Candidate:** Can work twenty hours per week.

**Agent:** Thank you. When would be your preferred start date for the internship?

**Candidate:** I need to correct that. I can only work ten hours per week.

**Agent:** I've noted the change. I currently have two different values for your weekly availability: 20 hours per week and 10 hours per week. Which one is correct?

**Candidate:** Correct one is ten hours per week.

**Agent:** Thank you for clarifying. Now, returning to the previous question: when would be your preferred start date for the internship?

**Candidate:** Sorry. I would like to again change my answer to the previous question. Actually, I can work for twenty hours per week.

**Agent:** I've noted that. I now have two different values for your weekly availability again: 10 hours per week and 20 hours per week. Which one is correct?

**Candidate:** Confirm twenty hours per week.

**Agent:** Thank you for confirming. Now, when would be your preferred start date for the internship?

**Candidate:** I can start immediately, and can you refer me to a human?

**Agent:** I have recorded that you can start immediately and have submitted a request for human assistance.

**Candidate:** Would like to stop here.

**Agent:** I understand. I have recorded the information you've provided so far and submitted your request for human assistance. Thank you for your time, and a team member will follow up with you.

## Assessment

This demonstrates both conflict handling and explicit human escalation without making an automated hiring decision.
