# Philippines Call 2 — Taglish, Affordability Objection & Payment Preference

## Scenario

A cooperative bancassurance customer completes qualification and discusses premiums, payment methods, affordability, and plan preferences.

## Localization demonstrated

- Filipino/Tagalog
- English
- Natural Taglish
- Polite "po" register
- Philippine financial/payment terminology
- Local payment methods

## Key test cases

### 1. Cooperative qualification

The customer provides:
- Age: 30
- Philippines residency: Yes
- Government-issued ID: Yes
- Partner-bank account: Yes
- Pre-existing conditions / long-term medication: No

### 2. Premium/payment question

Customer asks:

> "Can I ask how the premium works?"

The agent explains available payment methods, including:
- Auto-Debit Arrangement (ADA)
- E-Wallets
- Over-The-Counter payment
- Online Banking

### 3. Affordability objection

Customer states:

> "I'm a little worried, though, that I won't be able to afford the monthly premium."

The agent acknowledges the concern and discusses payment frequency and plan tiers.

**Result:** Affordability objection handled in Taglish/Filipino register.

### 4. Payment preference

The customer changes the preferred payment frequency to quarterly and specifies bank payment.

The agent records:

- Payment frequency: Quarterly
- Payment method: Bank account

### 5. Basic-tier preference

The customer asks whether the Basic Tier would generally be more affordable.

The agent explains the available tier information and records the preference.

### 6. Human escalation

Customer requests to speak with a Financial Advisor before deciding.

The agent routes the request to the Financial Advisor.

**Result:** Human escalation demonstrated while preserving the customer's language/register.

## ASR observations

These observations are retained as part of the prototype's known ASR limitations rather than being hidden.
