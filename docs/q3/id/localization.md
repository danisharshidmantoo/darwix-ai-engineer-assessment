# Indonesia Voice Bot — Localization Evidence

## Market

Indonesia

## Sector

Consumer finance / multifinance

## Language behavior

The prototype supports:

- Formal Bahasa Indonesia
- Colloquial Bahasa Indonesia
- Mixed Indonesian/English financial terminology

## Local financial terminology

The conversations use terms including:

- cicilan
- tenor
- denda
- DP
- jatuh tempo
- angsuran
- pembiayaan

## Localization examples

### Example 1 — Formal Indonesian

Call 1 uses formal customer-service language such as:

> "Selanjutnya, untuk keperluan administrasi..."

and:

> "Terima kasih informasinya."

### Example 2 — Colloquial Indonesian

Call 2 includes:

> "Saya umur tiga puluh tahun"

and:

> "enggak kuat kalau cicilannya terlalu besar."

This demonstrates colloquial conversation rather than direct translation of a formal script.

### Example 3 — Indonesian/English code-switching

Call 2 includes:

> "monthly payment"

within otherwise Indonesian speech.

This demonstrates natural use of English financial terminology within Indonesian conversation.

## Objection handling

The second call includes an affordability concern regarding the size of the monthly payment.

The agent acknowledges the concern and discusses DP and tenor.

## Fallback

When asked about changing the payment due date, the agent states that specific information was not available and recommends human assistance.

## Human escalation

Both calls demonstrate the ability to request human assistance.

## ASR limitations

The recordings show imperfect recognition of some Indonesian utterances, particularly colloquial and code-switched speech.

Examples include:

- incorrect recognition of colloquial expressions
- incorrect recognition of mixed-language financial phrases
- fragmented transcription of short utterances

These are documented as prototype limitations.

## Regional accent requirement

A separate regional-accent test has not yet been established from these two recordings.

The submission should not claim successful regional-accent testing unless an actual regional-accent recording is produced and evaluated.
