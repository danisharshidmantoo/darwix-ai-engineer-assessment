# Indonesia Call 1 — Formal Indonesian, Financial Terminology & Escalation

## Scenario

A cooperative consumer-finance customer completes the qualification flow and discusses financing preferences using formal Indonesian and local financial terminology.

## Qualification

The customer provides:

- Age: 30
- Indonesia residency: Yes
- Valid KTP: Yes
- Monthly income: Rp8,000,000
- Active personal bank account: Yes

## Financing discussion

The customer provides:

- DP: 20%
- Tenor: 24 months

The conversation includes Indonesian financial terminology including:

- DP
- tenor
- cicilan
- angsuran
- jatuh tempo

The customer asks about the calculation of a 24-month financing plan and asks when the installment due date occurs.

## Human escalation

The customer states that they understand the explanation but would like to speak with a human representative before continuing.

The agent routes the request to a human representative.

## ASR observations

The recording demonstrates several recognition issues, including:

- Short Indonesian utterances being split across multiple transcription events.
- Some English/Indonesian mixed utterances being partially recognized.
- The phrase concerning the 24-month calculation being transcribed incorrectly.

These are retained as observed prototype ASR limitations.
